## 1. EulerSwap AMM (swap)

**Impact:** MEDIUM

**Description:** EulerSwap is Euler's native AMM optimized for vault assets. Covers pool deployment using EulerSwapFactory, getting quotes with tick-based pricing and fee tiers, managing liquidity limits, and executing swaps (direct or via aggregators). Essential for on-chain liquidity provision.
