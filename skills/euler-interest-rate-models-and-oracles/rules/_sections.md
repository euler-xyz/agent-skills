## 1. Oracle Integration (oracle)

**Impact:** HIGH

**Description:** Oracle integration guide for Euler Finance V2 protocol. Covers deploying oracle adapters (Chainlink, Pyth, Uniswap TWAP, Chronicle, RedStone, Cross, Fixed Rate), configuring EulerRouter for price resolution, and querying asset prices. Essential for vault creation and price feed management.

## 2. Interest Rate Models (irm)

**Impact:** HIGH

**Description:** Available Interest Rate Models and their configuration. Euler supports multiple IRM types (Linear Kink, Adaptive Curve, Fixed Cyclical Binary, Base Premium) each suited for different use cases and risk profiles.
